Independent personal project

Price Monitor

A small personal project exploring product-offer monitoring and historical price analysis using publicly available marketplace information.

About the project

Price Monitor is intended for personal research and analysis. It helps organize product offers, compare retailer-level prices where data is available, and build a historical record of observed prices over time.

Planned capabilities

Offer comparison

Review available offers for selected products and retailers.

Price history

Record daily observations to understand price movements over time.

Data analysis

Export structured data for spreadsheet and business intelligence analysis.

Use and affiliation

This is an independent, non-commercial personal project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Idealo or any retailer. Any third-party data is subject to the relevant provider's terms and usage limits.
